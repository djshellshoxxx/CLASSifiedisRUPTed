## Requirements

- **PS-010-01.R01** A Negotiation links a buyer, a seller, a listing, an optional variant and the root Offer; each NegotiationRound is immutable and stores the signed statement digest.
- **PS-010-01.R02** Negotiation data is P2 and visible only to the two parties; rounds travel only as end-to-end encrypted envelopes and are never federated or sent to notification channels in plain form.
- **PS-010-01.R03** Timestamps in rounds are the signer's claims; ordering uses roundNo and the hash chain (prev), not clocks.

**Invariants**
- At most 10 rounds beyond the root offer and at most one non-terminal Negotiation per (buyerId, sellerId, listingId, variantId).
- status accepted implies dealId, confirmationCid and a verified seller confirmation exist; in the seller vault it also implies holdId with an active hold.
- buyerId differs from sellerId and awaiting is always the party that did not make the latest round.

**Invariants**
- A round is never modified or deleted while its negotiation exists.
- Unique (negotiationId, roundNo); a second different statement with the same number and signer marks the negotiation frozen.
- Rounds alternate proposers starting with the seller at round 1 when the root offer is the buyer's.
