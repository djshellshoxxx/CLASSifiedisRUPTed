## Requirements

- **PS-034-01.R01** An AuctionResult stores outcome, winner alias and commitment, final price, reserve check, ledger head, the cids of the public result and the disclosure, the vault's role, deal links per buyer, settlement state and second-chance data, with revision per CTR-ID-001.
- **PS-034-01.R02** The result is created once per auction by the host and mirrored read-only in participant vaults; participant-specific fields (role, deal ids, local state, host void application) are local.
- **PS-034-01.R03** Result visibility is tiered by two host-signed statements - the public classrup.auctions.result/v1 (outcome, final price, winner alias, winnerCommitment, ledger head) that every bidder verifies, and the P2 classrup.auctions.result_disclosure/v1 (winnerId and salt) end-to-end encrypted to the seller and the winner, who verify both.
- **PS-034-01.R04** finalPrice is the price of the ledger head entry at close; the close never computes a price of its own.

**Invariants**
- At most one AuctionResult per auction on the host and in each vault.
- outcome won implies winnerAlias, winnerCommitment, finalPrice and reserveCheck met or none; winnerId and winnerSalt exist only in host, seller and winner copies, and sha256(winnerId‖winnerSalt) equals winnerCommitment.
- finalPrice equals the price of the ledger head entry and is never lower than the reserve when a reserve exists and outcome is won.
- At most one secondChance per result, and the second-chance Deal's contextRef id differs from resultId.
- No vault applies a void over an acknowledged or final payment - state voided is set only when no Deal of this result in this vault had such a claim when the void was applied.
