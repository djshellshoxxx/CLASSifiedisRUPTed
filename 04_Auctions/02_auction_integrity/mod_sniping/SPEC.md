# F04.S02.M02 — Anti-sniping soft close

**Status:** SPECIFIED (Stage 3). **Scope:** MVP. **Parent:** [F04.S02](../SPEC.md). **Process IDs:** PS-038-01…PS-038-10. **Code:** `packages/auctions/src/auction_integrity/sniping`.

> Generated from `module.spec.yaml` by `tools/specgen`. Edit the YAML, then run `pnpm spec:render`.

## Purpose

Soft close on the host relay. Every accepted bid placed by a bidder in the final 2 minutes (120 000 ms, inclusive) of an auction moves the end 2 minutes later, up to 30 extensions, so the end can move by at most 60 minutes in total. This removes the last-second advantage of a hard close; field evidence on eBay (hard close) and Amazon (soft close) auctions found far more last-minute bidding under a hard close (Roth and Ockenfels, 2002, American Economic Review 92(4)). Soft close reduces but does not eliminate strategic late bidding, so the cap guarantees that every auction ends. The extension decision runs inside the bid transaction under the per-auction lease on the host, and every extension is a host-signed statement that bidders keep.

## Responsibilities

- Own the Extension record - one immutable row per extension - and the statement classrup.auctions.extension/v1 signed by the host key.
- Decide in the bid transaction whether an accepted bid qualifies - host time within 120 000 ms before currentEndsAt, strictly before the end, kind manual or proxy submitted by a bidder, extensionCount below 30.
- Update currentEndsAt through F04.S01.M01 adjust_end with compare-and-set on extensionCount, and emit auctions.auction.extended.
- Expose the rule constants and the extension history publicly so that bidders can predict and audit the end time.

## Not responsible for

- Bid acceptance and ordering (F04.S01.M02) and closing (F04.S01.M03); this module only decides and records extensions.
- Per-auction tuning of the window; the constants are fixed in rules version softclose-v1.
- Client countdown rendering beyond using host time offsets (shared UX in F14).
- Future: per-seller configurable windows and extensions proportional to bid activity.

## User stories

- As a bidder, I see that the auction can extend, how many extensions are left and the end time from the host's clock.
- As a bidder who was outbid in the last two minutes, I get time to respond because the end moved.
- As a seller, I know the auction can end up to 60 minutes after the scheduled end but never later.

## Edge cases

- A bid lands when exactly 120 000 ms remain; it qualifies. At 120 001 ms remaining it does not.
- Two bids are placed 50 ms apart inside the window; the lease orders them and each produces its own extension in sequence (the second extension counts from the already moved end).
- The 30th extension has been used; later bids in the window are still accepted but do not extend, and the UI shows final extension used.
- A proxy auto-raise generated by the host after a bid does not extend by itself; only the triggering bidder-submitted bid counts.
- A bid is rejected for a low amount inside the window; no extension is created.
- A retraction inside the window is refused by the bid module anyway (more than 12 h rule), and a retraction never extends.
- Host clock moves backward by an NTP step; the host keeps hostTime non-decreasing and uses the max of its clock and the last ledger time, so remaining time never grows because of a clock step.
- Bidder's phone shows the old end time; the bid receipt carries the new currentEndsAt and the UI updates from the receipt and the public history.

## UX and accessibility

- Countdown on the auction page: host-time based, text such as ends at 10:02 (may extend by 2 minutes per late bid, 28 of 30 extensions left).
- Extension notice toast and history list: each extension with time, new end and triggering sequence number.
- Bid receipt view: shows the end time after the bid and whether this bid extended the auction.

**States:** loading; running; in-final-window; extended; final-extension-used; closed

**Accessibility:** Extension notices are announced once through an aria-live polite region with the new end time as text.; The countdown is exposed as text updated at most every 10 s, and every 15 s inside the last minute.; Remaining extensions are a text count, not only a progress graphic.
