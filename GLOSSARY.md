# Glossary

- **ClassRup:** entire product.
- **LocalBazaar:** classifieds and direct commerce.
- **LocalMutiny:** bilateral and cyclic multi-party barter.
- **Cycle:** ordered trade graph where each participant gives and receives under a consented proposal.
- **Relay:** independently deployed service that routes/synchronizes signed records.
- **Noncustodial:** platform does not hold funds or private wallet keys.
- **Settlement evidence:** record of an assertion or externally verified result, not necessarily proof of physical delivery.
- **Geoprivacy:** minimization and controlled disclosure of location precision.
