## Method, provenance and limits

The primary lane inspected the original findings, current contracts, source
composition, logging/audit/metric ports, backup scope, federation/sync limits,
CI definitions, trace/status implementation, risk records and candidate evidence.
A separate read-only architecture reviewer checked all eight TD rows and their
source mappings. No product test, browser, scan or operational exercise was run
for this reconciliation. No live deployment, cloud configuration, operator access
or hosted repository-protection policy was inspected.

Read-only Git comparisons confirmed that the inspected `planning/`, `tools/`,
`packages/platform/`, workflow, decision/risk/dependency records, kernel
logger/metrics/bus/outbox/sync, matching and federation-scope foundations have
no diff from main at the reviewed candidate. The redactor vocabulary and the
recovery candidates are separately identified above. Documentation, specification
and source presence are not substituted for their required execution evidence.

The declared incident, response, relay backup and relay recovery paths were
checked specifically. Their absence is a statement about those paths in this
Git tree, not a claim about an unreviewed deployment or every conceivable
implementation location. The current README independently describes relay
delivery, full identity lifecycle and release scope as unfinished.

No role was assigned to a person, no deadline or exception was approved, and no
historical severity or gate was changed. The tracker remains the status authority;
this document supplies evidence and next acceptance boundaries for its 20 rows.
