## Continuation boundary

This mapping supplies inspection provenance and next bounded work; it is not a
new release approval or a change to the tracker's provisional process. The
original audits, tracker, risk register, runtime, tests, dependency pins and
workflow gates were left unchanged. Original evidence remains historical;
new evidence must identify its own source and executed scope. In particular,
record recovery and reason-preservation checks cannot close identity-device
recovery, distributed deletion, message confidentiality or settlement findings.
