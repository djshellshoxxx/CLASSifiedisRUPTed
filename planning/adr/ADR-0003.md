# ADR-0003 — Identifiers: UUIDv7 plus content IDs

**Status:** ACCEPTED. **Date:** 2026-11-04.

## Decision
UUIDv7 (RFC 9562) for record IDs: time-ordered for index locality, client-generatable for offline writes. Content IDs (`sha256-…` over RFC 8785 JCS) for signed/federated records. See CTR-ID-001.

## Consequences and notes
ULID rejected: no RFC; UUIDv4 rejected: poor index locality.
