# ADR-0006 — D03 Mixed-currency valuation

**Status:** ACCEPTED. **Date:** 2026-11-04.

## Decision
Integer minor units, pinned FX quotes with rational rates and expiry, HALF_EVEN rounding, tolerance rule in CTR-MNY-001 MNY-5. Valuations are participant-declared asking values, never platform appraisals; the platform only normalizes for comparison.

## Consequences and notes
Rate sources are adapters (F09.S03.M02 rates); without a configured source, cross-currency cycles require each party to declare values in the cycle settlement currency.
