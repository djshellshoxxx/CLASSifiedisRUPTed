# ADR-0008 — D05 Geoprivacy

**Status:** ACCEPTED. **Date:** 2026-11-04.

## Decision
Geohash cells with L0–L4 precision levels (CTR-PRV-001). Public max L2 (geohash 5). Distance buckets and query rate limits mitigate trilateration attacks documented against proximity apps.

## Consequences and notes
H3 rejected for MVP: extra WASM dependency without privacy gain at the chosen precision.
