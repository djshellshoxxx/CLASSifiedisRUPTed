# ADR-0014 — Module overlap resolution

**Status:** ACCEPTED. **Date:** 2026-11-04.

## Decision
Planning hierarchy truncated several compound names into sibling modules. Each pair now has a split responsibility recorded in `planning/modules.yaml`: mod_hours (quiet-hours schedule definition) vs mod_quiet (deferral/queue enforcement); mod_opt (channel opt-in/opt-out consent) vs mod_in (in-app notification preferences/categories); mod_feature (feature registry and dependency graph) vs mod_flags (per-tenant flag values/evaluation); mod_key (key generation/storage/rotation lifecycle) vs mod_management (key registry, revocation, distribution); mod_incident (detection, severity, records) vs mod_response (runbooks, containment, postmortems); F02 mod_party in trade_proposals (party roster of a proposal) vs mod_party in cycle_matching (participant eligibility for matching); currency modules: F09.S03.M01 owns the currency registry, F02.S04.M02 currency applies it to barter normalization, F14.S02 currencies owns display/formatting.

## Consequences and notes
No directory renamed, to keep registry IDs stable.
