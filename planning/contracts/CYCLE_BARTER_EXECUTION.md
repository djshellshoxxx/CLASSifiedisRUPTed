## Model and immutable proposal (CB-001)
A simple directed cycle transfers asset a_i from distinct principal p_i to p_(i+1 mod k), 2<=k<=8 in the bounded initial profile proposed for review. This profile uses exactly one listing per participant; >8-party and multiasset cycles remain tracked unresolved requirements, not approved deferrals or removed product scope. Outside the current profile return PROFILE_UNSUPPORTED without claiming the requirement complete. Within this profile, repeated principal/listing, missing edge, invalid quantities or disconnected subcycles reject VALIDATION_FAILED. Distinct keys do not prove distinct humans or ownership.
`Cycle {id,revision,created_by_principal_id,proposal_digest,status:draft|proposed|collecting_consent|ready_local|held_local|exchanging|reported_complete|cancelled|expired_local|disputed,expires_at,created_at,updated_at}`. Participant `{principal_id,slot,listing_ref:{origin_principal_id,id,economic_revision,economic_digest},quantity,receives_from_slot}`; evidence `{evidence_id,proposal_digest,signer_principal_id,decision:accept|decline|revoke,observed_at,signed_record_ref}`. Money obligation `{id,payer,payee,currency,amount_minor,purpose:balancing,evidence_refs}` is agreed responsibility, not custody/ledger.
Proposal digest covers version, all participant/listing economic revisions/digests, ordered edges, quantities/conditions, obligations, disclosure scope, deadlines and exchange terms. Canonical encoding/signatures await CR-001 reviewed crypto profile; cross-vault consent/import/execution disabled before approval. Material edit creates new digest and resets effective consent, retaining old evidence history. No signature of an object ID alone constitutes consent.

## Bounded local candidate search (CB-002)
Vertices are eligible listing/want combinations in the user's local approved projection; edges require recipient preference, category/condition/transport/disclosure constraints and local block rules. Recheck locally known eligibility before recording hold. Never infer private graph edges or query private inventories without explicit sharing consent. Deduplicate cycles by canonical rotation of stable origin principal/listing refs, sort deterministically.
Depth-first enumeration k2..8 capped at 500 vertices, 2000 edges, 50000 explored edges, 250ms CPU and 100 result cycles per request; worst-case exponential. Stop returns `{incomplete:true,reason:SEARCH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED}` rather than no-match claim. 2/3/4/N cycles share one validator. Rank constraint satisfaction and explicit compatibility only; no fair-price or global-optimality promise. Scope >8 and multiasset cycles require explicit bounded validation/search/consent contracts and review; remain unresolved tracked work, not approved scope reductions.

## States, consent and holds (CB-003)
`draft -> proposed -> collecting_consent -> ready_local -> held_local -> exchanging -> reported_complete`. Ready_local means all required valid acceptances of the exact digest are known to this vault, not simultaneity/global readiness. Unseen revocation remains possible. Any valid known decline/revoke dominates accept for that signer and exact digest, regardless of arrival order or untrusted timestamp, until a fresh proposal/digest and fresh unanimous consent. Reaccept of the same digest cannot revive readiness. Contradictory/forked signer histories quarantine that signer/proposal evidence and block advance; retain all evidence, never choose by arrival/time or presume consensus. Reviewed protocol must define causal history, terminal negative decisions and fresh-proposal binding; fixtures remain protocol-review blocked, not proof of distributed consensus. Decline/revoke before exchange invalidates local ready/hold; cancellation records evidence, releases own holds only. After handover, revocation/dropout opens disputed state and preserves evidence; no physical rollback. Proposal revision invalidates consent and requires new unanimous acceptance. Terminal states accept appended dispute/resolution evidence, never rewritten history.
In-process `cycle.propose`, `cycle.recordOwnConsent(digest,decision)`, `cycle.importConsent(record)`, `cycle.holdLocal(expected_revision,digest)`, `cycle.cancel`, `cycle.attestOwnHandoff`. Own consent must use unlocked named principal, exact current proposal, visible terms and explicit action; peer ACK cannot create consent. Imported evidence verification cannot impersonate parties. Errors: PROPOSAL_CHANGED, UNANIMOUS_CONSENT_MISSING, ITEM_STALE, RESOURCE_RESERVED_LOCAL, EXPIRED_LOCAL, CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, PROTOCOL_NOT_REVIEWED.
Hold transaction rechecks proposal digest/current local owner listing economic_revision/economic_digest/quantities plus local CAS revision, known revocations/local policy/clock and acceptances; creates only this vault's holds plus local CAS revision/receipt/outbox without changing signed economic revision/digest or invalidating that consent in one IndexedDB transaction. Foreign signed holds are evidence, not local ownership rights. No orchestrator with global lock authority, distributed atomic transaction, global inventory exclusivity or server-free atomic physical/money cycle. UI requires acknowledging sequential handover/dropout risk before recording an exchange.

## Mixed value (CB-004)
Only explicit mutually accepted obligations; no silently calculated debt. Per currency payer/payee amounts net to zero in proposal. The bounded initial profile proposed for review supports same-currency off-platform fiat assertions; FX, crypto compensation and other rails remain tracked unresolved requirements, not approved deferrals or scope deletion. Multi-currency quote fields `{quote_id,base,quote,rate_num,rate_den,source_id,observed_at,expires_at,max_age_seconds<=900}`; positive rational arithmetic in arbitrary precision, half-even once, display rounding deltas. Client clock cannot prove quote freshness; source and uncertainty must be shown. Chain/FX verification and crypto compensation require dedicated reviewed protocols before enablement; this blocker is not owner approval to omit them. Claimed/source-observed payments never automatically release another party's goods; confirmation may be revoked/reorganized.

## Failure, safety and UX (CB-005)
Own lease expiration/release is idempotent on next app resume or explicit check; background browser execution and synchronized clocks are not assured. Clock anomalies mark expiry uncertain and block auto-advance. Disconnected vault may commit local drafts/own assertions but shows peers unknown; no inferred acceptance or settlement. Dropout after physical handoff freezes local guidance, records dispute and offers user-controlled evidence export/off-app contact. Messaging remains disabled. No automatic reroute/substitution; new proposal needs new consent.
Local graph quotas and peer flood caps protect device resources, not global Sybil prevention. User-selected filters may hide peers locally. Present ordered transfers, current digest, per-party evidence provenance, known/missing/revoked consent, local hold scope, deadlines/clock caveat and independent payment states accessibly. No private graph/terms in diagnostics.
