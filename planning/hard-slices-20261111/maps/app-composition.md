## 9. Pitfalls
- Keep relative `.ts` imports in `apps/pwa/src` (bundle built from source; `allowImportingTsExtensions`).
- Tenant == principal DID; `restoreData` hooks reject foreign `ownerId`.
- Authz closes over `session` and `unlocked`; after `lock()` always rebuild via `connect`.
- `action` drops re-entrant calls silently; browser tests must wait on status text.
- `mutiny.alpha.consent` needs `policy.enabled`, an own reservation (`bazaar.inventory.reserve` then ingest its envelope), and still-admissible legs; a blocked counterparty yields `PRECONDITION_FAILED`.
- Trace JSON must come from vitest; Playwright and `node --test` output never count. `TEST_ID` is strict (`T5`/`T005`/`PS-21` do not match); skipped tests poison their ID.
