## 2. Money arithmetic rules (exact)

CTR-MNY-001: **MNY-1** `Money = { amount: string (decimal integer of minor units, optional leading '-'), currency: string }`; `bigint` in code; never floating point. **MNY-2** ISO 4217 minor units; `BTC` 8, `ETH` 18, `USDC` 6; unknown ⇒ `COMMERCE_CURRENCY_UNKNOWN`. **MNY-3** `HALF_EVEN` default, `FLOOR` only for fee caps; rounding once, at the end of a conversion. **MNY-4** quote `{ id, base, quote, rate: {num, den} (positive integers), source, asOf, expiresAt }`; `out = round(amount × num × 10^(dq) ÷ (den × 10^(db)))`; pinned by `id`; expired ⇒ `EXPIRED`, re-quote with fresh consent. **MNY-5** tolerance = 5 % of the larger side or a configured absolute minimum, whichever is greater; beyond ⇒ compensation leg or explicit acceptance of imbalance by the disadvantaged party. **MNY-6** mismatched currencies without a quote throws (`INTERNAL`).

PS-074: amount grammar `optional '-' then 1 to 38 digits without leading zeros (a lone 0 is allowed) and is not '-0'`; `+1`, `01`, `1.0`, `1e3` ⇒ COMMERCE_MONEY_INVALID; > 38 digits ⇒ COMMERCE_MONEY_OVERFLOW; rate num/den ≤ 40 digits; weights non-negative, ≤ 1000, sum > 0; `allocate(100 EUR,[1,1,1]) ⇒ ["34","33","33"]` (largest remainder, ties to lowest index, parts sum exactly); `round(-x) = -round(x)`; example `12345 EUR × 16312/100 → JPY = 12345×16312×10^0/(100×10^2) = 20137.164 → 20137`.

PS-075: provider decimal token → rational reduced by gcd (`1.0870 → 1087/1000`); lifetime default 900000 ms (provider 1–60 min, manual ≤ 24 h); staleness 96 h fiat / 5 min crypto ⇒ COMMERCE_RATE_STALE; > 50 % deviation in 24 h ⇒ COMMERCE_RATE_SUSPECT; never extended; pinned id must pass `commerce.fx_quote.check` (V08 ⇒ EXPIRED).

PS-025: `converted = round_half_even(declared × num × 10^dq ÷ (den × 10^db))` or `declared` when currencies are equal; fixture `10000 GBP × 117/100 → 11700 EUR`, `10990 CHF × 1043/1000 = 11462.57 → 11463`; ≤ 5 currencies, ≤ 8 quotes, 2..16 items; declared `> 0 and ≤ 1 000 000 000 000` minor; each pair needs a quote with ≥ 30 min life; `consentDeadline ≤ earliestQuoteExpiry`; `normalizationHash = sha256 of JCS(settlementCurrency, items, quotes)`; any recomputation difference ⇒ MUTINY_NORMALIZATION_MISMATCH.

PS-016 C05: `gap = |given − received| ≤ max(toleranceBps × larger side, absoluteMinRef converted to the settlement currency)`; `absoluteMinRef` default 500 EUR minor converted with the pinned quote HALF_EVEN (T18: `163/1 ⇒ 815 JPY`, `1/60000 ⇒ 8333 satoshi`); pass at the boundary, fail one unit above; excess covered only by a sealed compensation leg or a signed `acceptedImbalances` entry.

PS-026 planner: `n_i = received − given`; `tolerance_i = max(500 bps of larger side, 500 minor)`; debtors `n > tol`, creditors `−n > tol`; largest-first greedy, ties by DID ascending; ≤ `N − 1` legs (cap 8); amounts `1..1000000000000` minor in the settlement currency, no conversion, no rounding; sums conserved; `acceptedImbalance` participants omitted. Fixture 20000/14000/16000 ⇒ p2→p1 4000, p2→p3 2000. PS-027: `ratioPermille = floor(declared/referencePrice × 1000)`, same currency only; `< 200` low, `> 5000` high.

## 3. Focus 1 — IOX-2 preflight/dry-run and idempotent import

**IOX-2**: "Import validates format, signature, schemas (current or previous major), ID collisions (skip identical, reject conflicting), and never imports another user's private data; dry-run report before commit." **IOX-1** bundle: JSON Lines; manifest `{format, exportedAt, accountId, did, modules[], counts, sha256 per section}`, Ed25519-signed; then `{schema, record}` lines. No `modules.yaml` row owns import; PS-078 non-responsibility: "Importing bundles; the vault restore flow validates, dry-runs and commits (IOX-2)."

- PS-078-02.R02 "Each module section is validated against its registered schema list and its own line format before it is accepted."
- PS-078-09.R02 "Round-trip tests export a seeded vault and import it into a fresh one through the IOX-2 dry-run and commit path."
- PS-078-10.R02 "Import of a bundle is performed by the vault restore flow, which verifies the manifest signature and section hashes produced here before committing any record."
- PS-119-10.R01 "Records are exported by their owning modules (IOX-5); storage provides an ordered cursor scan per tenant and type and a restore that keeps IDs, revisions and timestamps and rejects conflicting copies." Import field: "repo.restore writes absent records as-is, skips identical ones and returns CONFLICT_REVISION for conflicting copies (IOX-2); blob refs of restored records are re-attached."
- PS-008-10.R02 "Import imports active holds as expired when their expiresAt has passed and never revives a lease; frozen holds stay frozen." Import field: "conflicting ids are rejected with a dry-run report."
- PS-023-10.R02 "Imported active reservations never revive holds; they are imported as expired unless the subject is re-confirmed and reserved again."
- PS-025-10.R02 "Import verifies hashes and arithmetic, keeps quote data exactly, and marks records whose quotes have lapsed as expired without altering them."
- PS-026-10.R02 "Import keeps amounts and statuses exactly as exported, never marks anything settled by itself, and asks F09 to re-sync claim status for due and claimed legs."
- PS-027-10.R02 "Import verifies signatures and chains, continues seq from the highest stored value and never imports a lock marker."
- PS-016-10.R02 "After import every imported subject is re-checked before any consent or proposal action is allowed."
- Also (text in file): PS-119-02.R01 (expectedRevision mismatch ⇒ CONFLICT_REVISION with currentRevision; 0 creates), PS-078-07.R02 (no other users' P2+, P4 or private keys), PS-078-10.R03 (previous major readable 180 days), PS-081-10.R02 (`redactForExport`; import re-classifies, unknown fields P3), PS-080-10.R02 (imported images re-stripped), PS-024-10.R02 (skip identical, reject conflicting), PS-018-10.R02 (re-verify before propose), PS-073-10.R02 (immutable-field conflict ⇒ COMMERCE_CURRENCY_IMMUTABLE), PS-075-10.R02 (past expiresAt ⇒ expired), PS-074-10.R01 (`parseMinor` on import).
